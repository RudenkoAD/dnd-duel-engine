# Arcane roster through level 5

All six alternatives use the class's existing spell slots, proficiency, casting ability, and level progression. The subclass starts at level 3. The fixed preparations spend the ordinary class allowance on arena spells and grant the subclass's supported spells separately.

| Roster preset | Feature or grant | Arena behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abjurer | Arcane Ward | A slotted abjuration creates the ward at twice Wizard level plus Intelligence modifier. Further slotted abjurations replenish twice the slot level. The recharge bonus action consumes a chosen slot. Damage reaches the ward before temporary HP. |
| Abjurer | Abjuration Savant | Fixed Savant entries are Alarm and Protection from Evil and Good (both rank 1 Abjuration) at Wizard level 3, then Counterspell (rank 3 Abjuration) at Wizard level 5. Protection from Evil and Good and Counterspell are in the corresponding prepared lists. |
| Diviner | Portent | Two privately rolled dice per duel. Before a visible creature's supported attack, save, or check, choose a die or let it roll normally. Portent replaces the d20 and is limited to one replacement per turn. Duel setup also offers Portent before initiative rolls. |
| Diviner | Divination Savant | Fixed Savant entries are Detect Thoughts and See Invisibility (both rank 2 Divination) at Wizard level 3, then Clairvoyance (rank 3 Divination) at Wizard level 5. See Invisibility is in the corresponding prepared list. |
| Aberrant Mind | Psionic Spells | Arms of Hadar, Calm Emotions, Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, and Mind Sliver at level 3. Hunger of Hadar at level 5. Detect Thoughts has a private Sense Thoughts result. Reading or probing private thoughts and Sending require a dialogue or thought system. |
| Aberrant Mind | Telepathic Speech | Out of scope for a duel with no language or dialogue actions. |
| Clockwork Sorcery | Clockwork Spells | Alarm, Protection from Evil and Good, Aid, and Lesser Restoration at level 3. Dispel Magic and Protection from Energy at level 5. Alarm has a ten-action setup, creature exemptions, audible and mental signals, and intrusion checks. |
| Clockwork Sorcery | Restore Balance | Reaction before a visible creature's d20 roll within 60 feet. Cancels advantage or disadvantage and spends one use from the Charisma-modifier pool. |
| Archfey | Archfey Spells | Calm Emotions, Faerie Fire, Misty Step, Phantasmal Force, and Sleep at level 3. Blink and Plant Growth at level 5. |
| Archfey | Steps of the Fey | Misty Step gets Charisma-modifier free casts. Both slot and free casts offer Refreshing Step or Taunting Step after successful teleportation. |
| Great Old One | Great Old One Spells | Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Phantasmal Force, and Tasha's Hideous Laughter at level 3. Hunger of Hadar at level 5. Detect Thoughts supports Sense Thoughts. Clairvoyance takes 10 minutes, beyond the match limit. |

Phantasmal Force represents an illusory hazard. After a failed Intelligence save, the caster chooses the visible lower corner and floor-relative height of a stationary 10-foot cube within range. During each of the caster's turns, the caster can deal 2d8 Psychic damage once if the known target stands inside the cube or within 5 feet of it. This choice spends neither an action nor a bonus action. The affected duelist can take the Study action to investigate the phantasm against the caster's spell save DC.
| Great Old One | Awakened Mind | Out of scope for a duel with no language or dialogue actions. |
| Great Old One | Psychic Spells | Select psychic damage for damaging Warlock spells. Enchantments and illusions omit verbal and somatic components while retaining material requirements. |
