# Bard and Ranger subclass coverage through level 5

| Preset | Feature | Duel implementation |
|---|---|---|
| College of Valor | Combat Inspiration, Defense and Offense | A recipient can spend the Bard's Inspiration on damage after hitting with an attack roll, or spend its reaction and roll the die to increase AC against an incoming attack. The recipient can still use the die for ordinary Bardic Inspiration. |
| College of Valor | Martial Training | Martial weapon proficiency, medium armor and shield training, and a held weapon spellcasting focus. The preset carries medium armor and a shield. |
| College of Glamour | Beguiling Magic | Adds Charm Person and Mirror Image. After a qualifying slotted Enchantment or Illusion spell resolves, choose a creature within 60 feet and either Charmed or Frightened. Targets make Wisdom saves on application and at the end of their turns. The first use is free. Bardic Inspiration can restore the feature on its own or when activating it again. |

Each gets twice one Inspiration die in temporary HP and can choose to move using its reaction without opportunity attacks. Movement uses a separate reaction allowance, normal terrain costs, standing from prone, and eligible climbing and flying steps. |
| Fey Wanderer | Dreadful Strikes | Optional 1d4 Psychic damage on a weapon hit, once per target per turn. |

## Spell and arena scope

- Charm Person targets the other humanoid duelist and rolls the save with advantage when the duelists are fighting. The Charmed condition blocks direct hostile targeting of its source. Damage from the caster's side ends the effect. Arena play has no separate NPC attitude system or eligible extra humanoid targets for upcasting.
- Mirror Image has a one-minute duration, three duplicates, and the 2024 per-duplicate d6 redirection on attack-roll hits. It bypasses attackers that cannot see or are already using Blindsight.
- Disguise Self records the hour-long illusion, exposes its duration state privately, can be seen through with a successful Investigation check, and can be dispelled. The fixed duel uses character tokens and has no appearance editor or social dialogue.
- Rope Trick uses a five-foot rope and the movement economy for climbing. A creature in the pocket can see out, while solid geometry and the planar boundary prevent outgoing and incoming attacks or spells. Occupants can pull up or lower the rope as an object interaction. Dispel Magic can close the portal. The duel uses a shared board footprint, so exit uses the base or an adjacent free supported cell. A creature of Medium size or smaller can climb in or out, including eligible familiars. Inside, duelists share one close-quarters position and use ordinary melee, ranged, self-cast, and creature-targeted combat rules. The pocket has no separate movement grid, placed-area spells, or rope damage.
- Bright layout IDs 0–3 and the canonical seed mapping are preserved. New roster encounters with a Gloom Stalker can select dark layout 4 by seed, and explicit arena selection also accepts layout 4. Ordinary opaque clouds block sight independently of darkness.
- Higher-level subclass features, campaign identity and rest actions are outside the level 1–5 encounter system.
