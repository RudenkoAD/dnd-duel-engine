# Level 5 Barbarian and Monk roster

The six presets use the 2024 Player’s Handbook. The existing Berserker and Open Hand remain the first entry in their class. The alternative paths differ through their subclass choices and keep each class’s existing Dwarf, standard-array allocation, background, level 4 Ability Score Improvement, and equipment.

| Build | Background and level 5 scores (STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA) | Equipment | HP / AC | Level 3 specialization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Berserker | Artisan, 19/14/14/8/12/10 | Greataxe, unarmored | 55 / 14 | Frenzy adds damage on a qualifying hit while raging and using Reckless Attack. |
| Zealot | Artisan, 19/14/14/8/12/10 | Greataxe, unarmored | 55 / 14 | Divine Fury adds 1d6 plus half Barbarian level to the first creature hit each own turn while raging. Choose Radiant or Necrotic. Warrior of the Gods gives a pool of four d12s and a bonus action to spend and roll one or more for healing. |

| Elements | Guide, 10/19/13/8/15/12 | Unarmed | 38 / 16 | At the start of a turn, spend 1 Focus Point for Elemental Attunement. Unarmed reach becomes 15 feet for up to ten minutes or until incapacitated. An Unarmed Strike may deal Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder. A qualifying strike can prompt a Strength save to move the target 5 or 10 feet toward or away from the Monk. The target may voluntarily fail. Manipulate Elements grants Elementalism using WIS. |

Elementalism uses a Magic action and verbal and somatic components. Pick a cube within 30 feet and a harmless effect. The arena depicts a breeze, surface dust, amber sparks with cinnamon scent, damp mist, or a cup of water on a surface. Sculpt Element can form a small figure from a suitable present effect or a small portion of a visible object flame. Sparks play as a brief event with scent for one minute, and the cup of water evaporates after one minute. Interactive doors and shutters, lightable candles and lamps, handwritten words on dirt, and free-form cosmetic input are outside the current board. The cantrip supports Ready and Counterspell and changes no damage, concealment, light, or terrain rules. Herbalism Kit proficiency is recorded. Arena combat has no active Herbalism Kit checks or crafting loop. The class setup also retains the existing common rest and duel-start assumptions, including full resources on entry.
