# Alternate Cleric, Druid, and Paladin builds

The roster uses the 2024 Player's Handbook. Features below unlock at class level 3. Granted spells unlock at their listed class levels and follow the arena's slot, hand, targeting, concentration, and reaction rules.

| ID | Subclass | Arena features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | War Domain Cleric | War Priest uses its own Wisdom-based pool for a bonus-action weapon attack or Unarmed Strike. Guided Strike can correct a missed attack by spending Channel Divinity, with a Reaction required when the beneficiary is another creature. |
| 8 | Trickery Domain Cleric | Blessing of the Trickster grants Stealth advantage to the chosen willing recipient. Invoke Duplicity uses Channel Divinity to place an intangible duplicate, which can be moved, dismissed, used as an optional casting origin, or swapped with the caster when placed or moved. Adjacent targets that can see the duplicate are distracted when also adjacent to the Cleric. |
| 10 | Circle of the Stars Druid | Star Map grants Guidance and Wisdom-based free castings of Guiding Bolt. Starry Form uses Wild Shape, lights the surrounding area, and offers Archer's radiant attack, Chalice's additional healing recipient, or Dragon's d20 floor for Intelligence and Wisdom checks and concentration saves. |
| 11 | Circle of the Sea Druid | Wrath of the Sea uses Wild Shape on manifestation. The player can strike on manifestation or as a bonus action while it remains active. One chosen creature within the spray makes a Constitution save against Wisdom-based cold damage. On a failure, a Large or smaller target can be pushed 0, 5, 10, or 15 feet. The emanation can be dismissed. |
| 19 | Oath of the Ancients Paladin | Nature's Wrath spends Channel Divinity after choosing one or more creatures within 15 feet. Targets make a Strength save and repeat it at the end of their turns until they succeed or the one-minute effect expires. |
| 20 | Oath of Glory Paladin | Inspiring Smite offers optional Channel Divinity immediately after Divine Smite and allows manual allocation of the temporary HP pool. Peerless Athlete grants Athletics and Acrobatics advantage and extends Long and High Jump distances while retaining ordinary movement costs. |

## Spell grants

| Subclass | Level 3 | Level 5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| War | Guiding Bolt, Magic Weapon, Shield of Faith, Spiritual Weapon | Crusader's Mantle, Spirit Guardians |
| Trickery | Charm Person, Disguise Self, Invisibility, Pass Without Trace | Hypnotic Pattern, Nondetection |
| Stars | Guidance, Guiding Bolt | |
| Sea | Fog Cloud, Gust of Wind, Ray of Frost, Shatter, Thunderwave | Lightning Bolt |
| Ancients | Ensnaring Strike | Misty Step, Moonbeam |
| Glory | Guiding Bolt, Heroism | Enhance Ability, Magic Weapon |

Water Breathing requires underwater terrain. Speak with Animals requires conversation with beasts. These two always-prepared spells appear as unavailable when the build is inspected. Nondetection applies to creature recipients since the arena has no divination targeting objects or locations. The Stars Druid starts with a stone Star Map in hand and the quarterstaff and shield stowed. Guidance and Guiding Bolt depend on holding the Star Map. Recovery on a rest and the Star Map replacement ceremony occur outside the single-duel simulation. Invocation of subclass abilities uses the arena's five-foot lattice and four active creature IDs. Guided Strike's reactions are offered for resolvable misses, and War Priest's weapon attacks use the arena's active equipment and creature targeting. Duplicity changes targeted spell origins while preserving the Cleric's own senses and the Cleric as the center of persistent self-centered emanations.
