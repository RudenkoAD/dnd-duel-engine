# Fighter and Rogue subclasses

These level-five roster presets use the Fighter or Rogue's shared origin, starting equipment, and ability scores. Subclass features unlock at level 3. The level 4 ability increase and level 5 class features continue to come from the base build.

| Roster ID | Preset | Creation choices and duel features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | Champion | Improved Critical on a weapon roll of 19 or 20. Remarkable Athlete gives advantage on initiative and Athletics checks and offers half-speed safe movement after a critical hit. |
| 13 | Battle Master | Student of War selects History and Smith's Tools. Combat Superiority starts with four d8s and selects Precision Attack, Trip Attack, and Parry. Maneuver save DC and Parry use the stronger of Strength and Dexterity. |
| 14 | Psi Warrior | Psionic Power starts with four d6s at levels 3–4 or six d8s at level 5. Protective Field is a damage reaction, Psionic Strike adds force damage on a qualifying weapon hit once per own turn, and Telekinetic Movement uses a Magic action. The first movement is free and subsequent uses spend a die. |
| 24 | Thief | Fast Hands supplies bonus-action equipment and caltrops interactions. Second-Story Work supplies climb speed and Dexterity-based jumping. |
| 25 | Assassin | Assassinate gives initiative advantage, attack advantage against combatants who have yet to act in the first round, and extra weapon-type damage equal to Rogue level when using first-round Sneak Attack. Tools of the Trade supplies Disguise Kit and Poisoner's Kit proficiency. |
| 26 | Soulknife | Psionic Power starts with four d6s at levels 3–4 or six d8s at level 5. Psi-Bolstered Knack can salvage a failed proficient skill or caltrops-disarming check and spends a die only when the check succeeds. Psychic Blades provide an optional melee or thrown attack, a melee opportunity attack, and a d4 bonus attack with the other hand. The primary blade deals d6 psychic plus Dexterity, has Vex, and throws to 60/120 feet across the board. Neither blade spends a psionic die. |

Precision Attack can repair a missed attack against a creature or an object. A creature hit produced by Precision Attack cannot use Trip Attack on the same attack. Psionic Strike can follow a damaging weapon hit against a creature or a surviving object. Trip Attack adds a d8 to damage and can knock a Large-or-smaller creature prone on a failed Strength save. Parry reduces damage from a melee attack by a die plus the chosen ability modifier and spends the Fighter's reaction.

Protective Field can reduce damage to the Fighter or a visible creature within 30 feet. It uses the same reaction as opportunity attacks and can respond to damage from attack rolls, spells, and hazards. The reaction reduces the raw damage total before damage-type resistance. For a hit with several damage types, the reduction is applied to the weapon or spell damage first, then to any additional damage.

Telekinetic Movement exposes visible unattended gear and other creatures within 30 feet. The opposing player must consent when the selected creature is under their control. The target and destination are chosen separately. Cancelling the choice preserves the action and die. Tiny daggers can move out of the caster’s hand or from the ground into a free hand. A creature placed in the air falls if it has no means of staying aloft.

The duel has no rest phase, social disguise checks, tool crafting, or player-to-player dialogue. Tool proficiencies are creation choices for those systems. Soulknife's Psychic Whispers would carry messages in a dialogue system. The hand model tracks occupied hands. When the first Psychic Blade used the only free hand, the Rogue must free the other hand before manifesting the second blade.
